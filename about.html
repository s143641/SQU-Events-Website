About SQU Events Hub

Our Idea

SQU Events Hub is a digital platform that helps students discover university events easily. It organizes academic, cultural, and social activities in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve student engagement and campus life by providing a simple and organized platform where students can explore events and register quickly.

Target Audience

The platform mainly targets SQU students who want to stay updated with university events and participate in activities that support their academic and personal growth.
